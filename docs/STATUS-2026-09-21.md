# Status, 2026-09-21 — the owner trades Polymarket US, not Polymarket

## Headline

The wallet importer shipped in `6baabed` **cannot see this owner's bets**, and the edge
math is tuned to the wrong venue's fee. Both were found by opening the owner's own
Polymarket account and reading the two exchanges' public APIs.

Polymarket is two separate products:

| | polymarket.com | polymarket.us |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Rest of world | US residents (regulated) |
| Identity | On-chain proxy wallet (`0x…`) | Account + API key, **no wallet at all** |
| Fills feed | `data-api.polymarket.com/trades?user=<wallet>`, keyless | `api.polymarket.us/v1/portfolio/activities`, **API key required** |
| Market data | `gamma-api.polymarket.com`, keyless | `gateway.polymarket.us/v1/...`, **keyless** |
| Taker fee coefficient | 0.05 | **0.0695** (every one of 814 markets on the game checked) |

The owner is in the US. polymarket.com shows an interstitial ("Trading is blocked in the
United States on polymarket.com") and their session there is signed out. Their real,
funded account is on polymarket.us.

## What this means, in order of cost

1. **`Prefs.pm_wallet` can never be filled in.** There is no wallet to paste. The .us
   profile page contains no `0x` address anywhere (checked: zero matches in the rendered
   DOM), and the account menu has no wallet entry — it has "Trading API" instead. The
   import feature is sound, it is simply pointed at an exchange this owner cannot use.
2. **The fee is wrong by 39%.** `prefs.taker_fee_rate` is 0.05; .us charges 0.0695. At a
   50c price that is 1.74c a share rather than 1.25c. `docs/DECISIONS.md` already records
   that a wrong fee manufactures edges — this is that failure mode, live. Until the venue
   question is settled, every edge the app reports is overstated by about half a point
   for anyone trading .us.
3. **Market identity differs.** .us markets are keyed by `marketSlug`
   (`asc-nfl-nyg-lar-2026-09-21-pos-6pt5`), not by a `conditionId` and CLOB token ids.
   Nothing in `Market` or `Bet` maps onto them today.

## What is NOT a problem

The two venues price the same game the same. On the Monday night game the app scanned,
.com had Rams -6.5 at 0.53 and .us has the same market at ~0.53. So the .com-based
consensus is a fair read of what .us is charging; it is the fee and the ledger that are
wrong, not the prices.

## What a .us importer needs (verified against the live docs)

`GET https://api.polymarket.us/v1/portfolio/activities`

- Headers: `X-PM-Access-Key` (key id, a UUID), `X-PM-Timestamp` (ms, must be within 30s of
  server time), `X-PM-Signature` (base64 Ed25519 over `timestamp + method + path`).
- Query: `limit`, `cursor`, `marketSlug`, `types` (use `ACTIVITY_TYPE_TRADE`), `sortOrder`.
- Returns `activities[]`; a trade carries `id`, `marketSlug`, `state`, `createTime`,
  `price{value,currency}`, `qty`, `costBasis`, `realizedPnl`, and **`isAggressor`** —
  which is maker-vs-taker, the one thing the .com feed could not tell us. Paged by
  `nextCursor` / `eof`.
- The key is created by the owner at `polymarket.us/developer` after identity
  verification, and the secret is shown once. It is a credential: it belongs in `.env`,
  never in source, and nobody but the owner should ever generate it.

Public market data needs no key: `gateway.polymarket.us/v1/events?limit=`,
`/v1/events/slug/<slug>`, `/v1/markets?limit=`, `/v1/search?q=`. An event carries every
market inline with `outcomes`, `outcomePrices`, `bestBidQuote`, `bestAskQuote`,
`feeCoefficient`, `orderPriceMinTickSize`, `minimumTradeQty`, `gameStartTime` and a
`sportsMarketType` (`football_team_full_game_spread`, `..._full_game_total`,
`..._full_game_winner`, and a long tail of halves, quarters and player props).

**Parser warning for whoever builds this.** The outcome labelling is genuinely confusing:
the market titled "Los Angeles Rams wins by over 6.5 points" has `question` "Will the New
York Giants cover 6.5…" and `outcomes` `["-6.50","+6.50"]`. Do not trust the sign in the
label. Anchor the side on the price against a known reference (the owner's own entry, or
the .com book) before trusting any mapping, and put the convention in the scan notes the
way `HOME_AWAY_CONVENTION` already is.

## Decision the owner has to make

This is not a coding choice, so it is not made here:

- **Trade .us** (the funded account). Then the app needs a .us client for market data,
  the fee set to 0.0695, and a key-based importer. Bigger job, matches reality.
- **Keep the app read-only against .com** and log .us bets by hand. Cheapest, but the
  ledger stays manual and the fee still needs correcting.

Either way `taker_fee_rate` should stop saying 0.05 for anyone trading .us. It was left
at 0.05 rather than changed here, because which value is right depends on the answer
above and the setting drives money decisions.
